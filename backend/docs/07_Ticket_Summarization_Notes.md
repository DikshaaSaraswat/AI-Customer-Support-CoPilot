# Ticket Summarization

## Objective

The objective of this module is to automatically generate concise summaries of customer support tickets.

Support tickets are often lengthy and contain unnecessary details. Summarization helps customer support agents quickly understand the customer's issue without reading the complete ticket.

The summaries generated by the AI Copilot are intended to assist human support agents and improve response efficiency.

## Model Selection

The project uses **Google FLAN-T5 Small**, a lightweight instruction-tuned Large Language Model (LLM).

Reasons for selecting FLAN-T5 Small:

- Lightweight and CPU-friendly
- Produces high-quality abstractive summaries
- Easy to integrate using the Hugging Face Transformers library
- Suitable for internship-scale projects

# Ticket Summarization

## Objective

The ticket summarization module aims to generate a concise summary of long customer support tickets. This helps support agents quickly understand the customer's issue without reading the entire ticket.

---

## Model Selection

Initially, Google FLAN-T5 Small was evaluated for ticket summarization.

Although FLAN-T5 is a powerful instruction-tuned language model, the generated summaries were not sufficiently concise or accurate for customer support tickets.

Therefore, the project uses **Facebook BART Large CNN**, which is specifically fine-tuned for abstractive text summarization.

### Reasons for selecting BART

- Specifically designed for summarization tasks.
- Produces concise and meaningful summaries.
- Widely used in NLP applications.
- Better suited for customer support ticket summarization than general-purpose language models.


## Initial Observation

The BART Large CNN model successfully generated concise summaries by extracting the main issue from customer support tickets.

Compared to FLAN-T5 Small, BART produced more focused and meaningful summaries, making it more suitable for customer support ticket summarization.


### Sample Result

**Original Ticket:**
The customer reported that the centralized account management portal was offline, preventing access to account settings. The customer also mentioned trying multiple browsers and devices and requested an update on the outage and alternative access methods.

**Generated Summary (BART):**
"The centralized account management portal appears to be offline. This outage is blocking access to account settings, leading to substantial inconvenience."

**Observation:**
The generated summary successfully captures the core issue while removing greetings and unnecessary details, making it easier for support agents to quickly understand the customer's problem.


## Model Evaluation

The BART Large CNN model was evaluated on multiple customer support tickets.

### Observations

- Successfully identified the primary issue in customer support tickets.
- Removed greetings and unnecessary introductory text.
- Generated concise summaries that retained the core customer concern.
- Performed consistently across different ticket categories, including technical issues, product inquiries, and service requests.

### Conclusion

Based on the experimental results, Facebook BART Large CNN was selected as the final summarization model for the AI Customer Support Copilot due to its ability to generate concise and meaningful summaries suitable for support agents.

### Implementation

A reusable Python function (`summarize_ticket()`) was created to generate concise summaries using the selected BART Large CNN model. This function will later be integrated into the AI Customer Support Copilot dashboard to automatically summarize incoming customer tickets.
